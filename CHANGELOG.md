# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First independent plugin release, compatible with hz 0.7.2 through the declared `<1` bound.
- The winter theme includes the checked avatar palette, snowy cap and frosty scarf.
- Snow and Fireplace are packaged YouTube stream choices, with Snow as the sole Follow my theme pairing.
- Existing sound identifiers remain available. No media is redistributed and playback still requires Play.
